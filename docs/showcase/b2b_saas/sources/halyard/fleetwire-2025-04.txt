Telematics market: the challengers gain share

Independent telematics providers keep winning fleets from the vehicle makers' own systems, according to the latest quarterly survey by the Telematics Index, an industry tracker.

The tracker counts connected commercial vehicles by provider in Ireland, the UK and the Benelux. It says the market grew 14% in 2024.

Dublin-based Halyard Fleet was among the fastest-growing providers in the survey.

According to the Telematics Index, Halyard had about 44,000 vehicles tracked at the end of 2024, which puts it in the top five of the independent providers.

Analysts expect the vehicle makers to answer with lower prices for their own systems in 2025.
