Pinewick Security opens Boston office as US demand for identity security grows

Berlin-based Pinewick Security said its ARR reached EUR 45 million at the end of 2024, in a statement announcing a new US office in Boston.

The company sells software that detects attacks on user identities across cloud and on-premise directories. Its customers include banks, insurers and manufacturers in Germany and the Nordics.

The chief executive said in the statement that the company was close to cash-flow break-even and would fund the US expansion from its own cash.
