Kestrel Ledger hires new sales chief as it eyes the Nordics

Munich-based Kestrel Ledger, which sells accounts-payable and month-end close software to mid-sized companies, has hired a chief revenue officer from a large accounting-software vendor. The company plans to open a Stockholm office this year.

Kestrel Ledger ended 2024 with annual recurring revenue of EUR 15 million, according to two people familiar with the business. The company declined to comment on its financials.

The company competes with the finance modules of the large ERP vendors and with a group of venture-backed start-ups. Customers say the product is quick to implement and connects well to the ledgers used in Germany.

Kestrel Ledger last raised money in 2022. People close to the company say it is now talking to growth investors about a Series B round.
